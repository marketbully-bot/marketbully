03 The hard question

“What is your accuracy?”

Everybody asks it and they should. Here is my straight answer, including the part most people in this business will not tell you.

I do not publish a win rate

Not because it is bad. Because I cannot prove it, and neither can anybody else quoting you one. I have no audited account behind me. The people showing you ninety percent on a graphic do not have one either. A number nobody can check is worth exactly nothing, and if I put one up you would be right not to believe it.

So I am not going to insult you with one.

Here is what I give you instead

Watch me trade it live, for free, before you give me anything at all. Every call I make comes with the level and the reason attached, so you can follow the logic yourself instead of taking my word for it.

If the thinking does not hold up when you watch it, you will see that for yourself, and you should walk. That is a better test than any percentage on a sales page.

02 The work

How I actually teach it

Not tips and not screenshots after the fact. A repeatable way to mark a chart, find the level that matters, and know your risk before the candle prints.

The MB Strategy

Fibonacci settings I adjusted over years to fit how I actually read a chart, combined with market structure, supply and demand, and volume metric order blocks. Not a secret indicator. A set of rules for where price reacts and what you do when it gets there.

The Olympus Scanner

My own scanning tool. It surfaces the conditions I am looking for so I am not flipping charts all session. Members learn what it flags and, more importantly, when to ignore it.

Taught in order

Handing somebody a settings screenshot without the reasoning underneath just gets them into worse trades faster. So it comes in pieces, in sequence, and the reasoning comes first.

FAQ

Straightanswers

Including the money questions, which most sites in this space bury.

Is the signals chat really free?

Yes, and it stays free. There is no subscription for the chat. What it requires is opening a trading account through my link, because that is how the room gets paid for.

How do you get paid, exactly?

Two ways. My broker pays me a rebate based on the trading volume of people who signed up through my link. 1House Global pays me a referral commission when someone enrols through my link, and it recurs while they stay subscribed.

The honest implication: I earn more when members trade more, regardless of whether those trades win. Read anything I post with that in mind.

Do I have to use your broker?

To get into the gated chat, yes. That is the condition of entry and I say it up front rather than after you have applied. To learn from me, no. The sessions on 1House.TV are there whether you ever open an account or not.

What should I know before I fund an account?

Read the broker risk section on the legal page first. It sets out the regulatory position in plain language, including what protections you do not have. Search the broker's name yourself as well. Then decide, and only deposit what you could lose entirely without it changing your life.

Is this financial advice?

No. I am not a registered investment adviser and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument. It is education and commentary on my own trading. Your account, your decisions, your risk.

What is 1House and do I have to pay for it?

1House.TV is the streaming platform where my sessions are published. There is a free way to watch, and there are paid plans run by 1House Global, not by me. You do not need a 1House plan to be in the signals chat.

How long does approval take?

I review applications myself, usually the same day. If you are declined you will be told, and it is not personal. The room works because it stays small enough that I know who is in it.

Who does not get approved?

People who cannot fund an account, because the room will not do anything for them. And people who show up to argue or to recruit for their own group. Both are easy to spot in the application.

Can I share my invite with a friend?

It will not work. Every invite is made for one person and expires the moment it is used. If you want somebody in, send them to the application and they go through the same door you did.

Can I get a refund?

There is nothing to refund on the chat, it is free. Anything you pay 1House Global or deposit with a broker is governed by their terms, not mine, and you should read those before you pay.
